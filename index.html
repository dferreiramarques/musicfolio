About

b. 10 March 1980 — Porto, Portugal

David Marques started making music in the early 2000s, when the distance between a four-track recorder and a stage felt like the only map worth following. What emerged wasn't a genre but a method: work with what the project demands, not what the format allows.

Each alias carries its own logic. s0ma operates in dense electronic terrain; Zarhana folds the Portuguese guitar into something more unsettled; Megateddy anchors itself in rock with the patience of a band that has read Velvet Underground and decided not to rush. b1tn1k is the one-man-band taken seriously — no irony, just accountability.

Across all of it, the throughline is less about instrument or style than about how sound is used: as structure, as atmosphere, as argument.

Based in Porto, Portugal.

Collaborate

Open to hearing what you're working on — finished, half-formed, or just an idea with nowhere to go yet.

  • listen to your music and talk about where it could go
  • define something new together, without a genre in mind
  • improvise — in a room, remotely, or somewhere in between
  • produce, arrange, or strip things back
  • build a live set for a specific moment, then dissolve it

Not looking for a band. Looking for people who treat a project as a question worth asking — for as long as it takes to answer it.
